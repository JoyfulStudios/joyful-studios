Get to know my why

I believe that I was created by the Creator to create.
I seek to follow Jesus in everything I do.

I picked up a camera at the age of 14 and haven’t really stopped since. My love for photography has grown out of a love for making things beautiful. I strive for every photo to feel alive - full of intention, emotion and feeling.

My work is inspired by the nostalgia of old films with lots of warmth, raw emotion and dreamy light. I prefer this to a rigid, ultra-polished approach and I rather seek to document the moment as it’s felt.

Photos are such a unique gift because you never know when you might have this moment again - and I think that every season of life is worth capturing.

What it’s like to work with me

You don’t need to know how to pose

Most people tell me, “I’m not photogenic” or “I don’t know what to do with my hands.” That’s completely normal, and it’s my job, not yours.

I don’t stand you in stiff poses. Instead, I give you simple prompts to react to, like:

  • Whisper something silly in your partner’s ear
  • Walk toward me slowly
  • Turn to one another and touch forehead to forehead
  • Tuck your hair behind your ear and look down

The result is natural, and relaxed. I aim to tell a story through photos, but also to document moments as they unfold.

One of my top priorities is making sure my clients are relaxed, comfortable and having fun.

For weddings, my aim is to be as unobtrusive as possible. It’s your day, and I’m privileged to be there to document it. I’ll offer gentle guidance during portraits and group photographs, and thoughtfully bring a little order to the chaos when needed. But I’m never there to orchestrate every moment. I want you to experience your wedding day fully, knowing that the meaningful moments, big and small, are being captured along the way.

Joy is a wedding, couples & lifestyle photographer capturing film-inspired photos and special moments.
