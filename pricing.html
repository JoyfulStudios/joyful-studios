Creator & Influencer & Family & Lifestyle

These details apply to the Creator & Influencer and Family & Lifestyle subscriptions.

Your sessions

Your included sessions are planned 20-minute sessions, styled around the monthly concepts, themes, seasons or photography opportunities I share with you. Twenty minutes keeps things relaxed and easy to fit into your life.

A 40-minute session isn’t a standard monthly session unless your subscription lists it as a benefit (as the Priority and Pro Creator plans do). Where your plan includes one, it’s booked in advance and is ideal for birthdays and special events, a particular location, a more involved lifestyle session or a specific content need.

Monthly planning & booking

At the beginning of each month you’ll receive an email with that month’s styled sessions, concepts and locations. Reply to choose the session(s) you’d like to attend.

Sessions have limited availability and are booked first-come, first-served. Your subscription guarantees you access to the monthly booking opportunity, but not a place in one particular session, date, location or concept, so book early, especially for 40-minute sessions, specific locations, birthdays and events.

If you’ve confirmed a session and can’t make it, please let me know as soon as you can. Without reasonable notice, the session may be treated as used.

Bring friends or collaborators

For an additional fee equal to 20% of your monthly subscription, you can include up to 5 additional people in a single session: a friends’ photoshoot, a creator collaboration, a group content session or a small friendship group.

Please arrange this in advance and let me know before the session. For larger groups, or sessions that become more complex, I may suggest a longer session or a separate quote. Additional people don’t become subscribers, so they don’t receive credits or booking rights.

Session credits

If you don’t use an included session in a month, it carries forward as a session credit. You can hold up to 3 unused sessions at any time; beyond that, extra unused sessions are forfeited.

Credits and sessions have no cash value and can’t be refunded, exchanged or transferred to someone else.

Payment

Subscriptions are paid monthly, on a payment date we agree when you join. Each month’s payment is made before that month’s sessions and benefits are used.

If a payment is missed, the subscription may be suspended: no new sessions are scheduled and no new galleries are delivered until the account is up to date. Photographs you’ve already received are always yours to keep.

Changing or cancelling

You can ask to change your subscription level at any time. Changes are subject to availability and take effect once confirmed in writing.

You can cancel with 30 days’ written notice. A cancellation fee of 50% of your total subscription fees over the agreed term applies; it covers planning, scheduling and the availability reserved for you. For example, a 6-month subscription at R350 a month (R2,100 in total) would carry a cancellation fee of R1,050.

Your photographs

Professionally edited photographs from standard 20-minute sessions are delivered within 10 business days, usually through an online gallery. If a gallery is especially large, I’ll let you know about any delay.

Please download and safely store your photographs once your gallery arrives. Galleries aren’t kept online permanently and gallery links may expire.

Locations, travel & weather

Sessions within 40 km of Durbanville, Cape Town are included. Further than that, a travel fee applies and is confirmed with you before the session. Any location costs such as entrance fees, parking or permits are paid by the client.

If the weather makes an outdoor session impractical or unsafe, we’ll reschedule it, and that won’t count as a missed session.

The Wedding Journey & The Growing Family

These details apply to The Wedding Journey and The Growing Family subscriptions. Where something applies to only one, it’s labelled.

How your subscription worksBoth

Wedding Journey: rather than booking each session or event separately, you commit to a subscription period of 6, 12 or 24 months and receive the photography services of your chosen tier: Essential, Celebration or Love Story.

Growing Family: this isn’t simply a set of individual photoshoots, but a continuing visual record of your family’s story over 12 or 24 months. You don’t need to know your session dates when you sign; we plan each stage together as your family’s season unfolds.

Booking & your wedding dateWedding Journey

All sessions and wedding dates are subject to my availability, and a subscription doesn’t guarantee availability on every date you request. Please share your preferred dates as early as you reasonably can.

Reserve your wedding date as early as possible. Once I’ve confirmed it in writing, that date is reserved for you under the terms of your agreement.

Wedding coverageWedding Journey

The hours in your tier refer to photography coverage, not necessarily the total length of your wedding day. Extra hours can be added at my current additional-hour rate, subject to availability.

Travel outside my standard service area may carry a travel fee, which I’ll confirm before your booking is confirmed.

Engagement & additional sessionsWedding Journey

Where your tier includes an engagement session or additional photoshoot, we’ll schedule it at a mutually agreed date and location, within your active subscription period unless we agree otherwise in writing.

Additional sessions can be booked separately at my current rates.

Your family’s timelineGrowing Family

Your sessions follow the seasons of your family: pregnancy announcement, maternity, newborn, then young family. The exact timing depends on your circumstances, and we plan each stage together.

What counts as a sessionGrowing Family

A session is one photography experience of the approximate length shown in your plan. It can take place at your home, outdoors, in a studio or at another agreed location.

Location fees, entrance fees, studio hire, travel outside my standard service area and other third-party costs aren’t included unless stated.

Newborn photographyGrowing Family

Newborn sessions are planned around your baby’s needs, with a calm, safe, baby-led approach. I won’t perform any pose or action I reasonably consider unsafe, and parents or guardians remain responsible for their child throughout the session.

PaymentBoth

Your subscription is paid monthly for the full term, on the payment date we agree when you sign.

Wedding Journey: a subscription is not a credit facility. If payments are overdue, services may be paused, subject to applicable law and your agreement.

Rescheduling & missed sessionsBoth

With reasonable notice, sessions can be rescheduled, subject to my availability. If weather affects a session, it moves to another mutually suitable date.

Growing Family: if a session needs to move because of illness, pregnancy-related circumstances, your baby’s needs or another reasonable circumstance, I’ll work with you to find a new date, and genuine family and pregnancy circumstances are always considered sympathetically.

If a confirmed session is repeatedly missed without reasonable notice, it may be treated as used, where reasonable and lawful.

CancellationBoth

You can cancel in line with South African consumer law and the rules for fixed-term consumer agreements, by giving written notice. If you cancel before the end of your term, any cancellation fee or other amount payable is determined in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances. Nothing in your agreement limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act or other applicable law.

I may end the agreement if there is a material breach that isn’t put right within a reasonable period after written notice.

Wedding Journey: if I’m unable to fulfil a confirmed booking for reasons within my control, I’ll make reasonable efforts to provide a suitable alternative, including (with your agreement) a replacement photographer. If no suitable alternative is accepted, you’ll receive the remedy required by the circumstances and the law. I’m not responsible for failure to perform caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control, such as serious illness, natural disasters, venue closure or government restrictions.

Expiry & renewalBoth

Your subscription ends at the end of the agreed term unless it’s renewed or continued in line with applicable law. I’ll contact you before it expires with your renewal options, and any renewal is subject to the pricing and terms that apply at the time.

Your photographs & copyrightBoth

Final photographs are professionally edited and delivered through an online gallery. You may download and share them for personal use, and with a Wedding Journey subscription you also receive a personal, non-exclusive licence to print them.

Copyright remains with Joyful Studios unless we agree otherwise in writing. Wedding Journey: photographs may not be sold, commercially licensed, substantially altered or used for commercial purposes without written permission.

Photographic styleBoth

I work in a film-inspired, documentary and cinematic style, aiming to capture authentic moments and the character of your story rather than identical or heavily posed photographs. Your final gallery will reflect my artistic style and creative judgement, including composition, lighting, editing and image selection.

Portfolio, children & privacyBoth

You choose whether I may use photographs from your sessions for my portfolio, website and social media. This is part of your agreement, and you can withdraw permission for future use by contacting me in writing.

Growing Family: where photographs include children, you confirm that you’re authorised to consent to their participation.

Personal information is used only to administer your subscription, communicate with you, arrange sessions and deliver your photographs, and is handled in line with POPIA where applicable.
