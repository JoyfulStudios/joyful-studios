Frequently Asked

Do you help with posing and directing?

Yes, gently. I use simple prompts and guidance during your session so you feel relaxed and the photos still look natural, never stiff or staged.

Do you offer engagement or pre-wedding photoshoots?

Yes. Engagement and couples sessions are booked at my standard rate of R1,900 per hour (minimum booking of 1 hour), and can happen any time before your wedding day.

Do you offer videography as well?

My focus is photography. If you’d like video alongside your photos, get in touch and we can talk it through.

When will we receive our photographs?

Your photographs are delivered in a private online gallery where you can view, download and share them. I’ll confirm the delivery timeframe with you when you book.

What happens if our wedding plans change?

Life happens. Get in touch as soon as you can and we’ll work out the best way forward together. The terms for changing a date are covered in your contract.
