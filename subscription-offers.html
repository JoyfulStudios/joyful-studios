Subscription Offers

Joyful Studios subscriptions are designed for people who want to document their lives over time - whether you’re building a personal brand, watching your family grow, or beginning a new chapter together.

Choose your subscription period, enjoy your complimentary first shoot, and receive ongoing opportunities to create beautiful, meaningful photographs throughout the year.

How it works

01 Choose your Subscription

Choose the subscription that fits you - whether you’re a creator, family or couple. The longer you commit, the more sessions and flexibility you receive.

02 Choose a commitment period

Choose a commitment period of either 6, 12, or 24 months, depending on your kind of subscription.

03 Get Your Complimentary Shoot

Every new subscriber receives a complimentary 40min photoshoot when they join. Consider it your first introduction to the Joyful Studios experience.

04 Watch your Inbox

At least once a month, subscribers will have the opportunity to book a short photoshoot on a Saturday morning or late afternoon. You’ll receive an email with all the details - including the date, location, concept and style guide.

OR book your Sessions

05 Choose your Shoot

For families, monthly opportunities will generally be styled/location-based shoots. You can choose whether you’d like to join that month’s session.

For creators, there will be occasional styled content shoots, but you can also book your own sessions in advance depending on your subscription level.

06 Show up

Most subscriber sessions will be around 20 minutes, keeping them relaxed, easy to fit into your schedule and focused on creating a beautiful set of images.

Higher-tier subscriptions receive more flexibility and time, including the ability to book their sessions further in advance and choose dates that work for them.

Good to know

The practical details of the Creator & Influencer and Family & Lifestyle subscriptions. You’ll receive the full subscription agreement to read and sign before you begin.

Your sessions

Your included sessions are planned 20-minute sessions, styled around the monthly concepts, themes, seasons or photography opportunities I share with you. Twenty minutes keeps things relaxed and easy to fit into your life.

A 40-minute session isn’t a standard monthly session unless your subscription lists it as a benefit (as the Priority and Pro Creator plans do). Where your plan includes one, it’s booked in advance and is ideal for birthdays and special events, a particular location, a more involved lifestyle session or a specific content need.

Monthly planning & booking

At the beginning of each month you’ll receive an email with that month’s styled sessions, concepts and locations. Reply to choose the session(s) you’d like to attend.

Sessions have limited availability and are booked first-come, first-served. Your subscription guarantees you access to the monthly booking opportunity, but not a place in one particular session, date, location or concept, so book early, especially for 40-minute sessions, specific locations, birthdays and events.

If you’ve confirmed a session and can’t make it, please let me know as soon as you can. Without reasonable notice, the session may be treated as used.

Bring friends or collaborators

For an additional fee equal to 20% of your monthly subscription, you can include up to 5 additional people in a single session: a friends’ photoshoot, a creator collaboration, a group content session or a small friendship group.

Please arrange this in advance and let me know before the session. For larger groups, or sessions that become more complex, I may suggest a longer session or a separate quote. Additional people don’t become subscribers, so they don’t receive credits or booking rights.

Session credits

If you don’t use an included session in a month, it carries forward as a session credit. You can hold up to 3 unused sessions at any time; beyond that, extra unused sessions are forfeited.

Credits and sessions have no cash value and can’t be refunded, exchanged or transferred to someone else.

Payment

Subscriptions are paid monthly, on a payment date we agree when you join. Each month’s payment is made before that month’s sessions and benefits are used.

If a payment is missed, the subscription may be suspended: no new sessions are scheduled and no new galleries are delivered until the account is up to date. Photographs you’ve already received are always yours to keep.

Changing or cancelling

You can ask to change your subscription level at any time. Changes are subject to availability and take effect once confirmed in writing.

You can cancel with 30 days’ written notice. A cancellation fee of 50% of your total subscription fees over the agreed term applies; it covers planning, scheduling and the availability reserved for you. For example, a 6-month subscription at R350 a month (R2,100 in total) would carry a cancellation fee of R1,050.

Your photographs

Professionally edited photographs from standard 20-minute sessions are delivered within 10 business days, usually through an online gallery. If a gallery is especially large, I’ll let you know about any delay.

Please download and safely store your photographs once your gallery arrives. Galleries aren’t kept online permanently and gallery links may expire.

Locations, travel & weather

Sessions within 40 km of Durbanville, Cape Town are included. Further than that, a travel fee applies and is confirmed with you before the session. Any location costs such as entrance fees, parking or permits are paid by the client.

If the weather makes an outdoor session impractical or unsafe, we’ll reschedule it, and that won’t count as a missed session.
